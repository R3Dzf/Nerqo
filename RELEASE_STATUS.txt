Nerqo native 1.0.8 Stable channel

Single-pass Windows snapshot preparation with SHA-256 and progress, a saved
incoming upload queue, and separate persistent History on Windows and Android.
Windows outgoing deliveries require best-effort verified-client receipts;
old shares are not imported as completed transfers. Android history upserts
paused/resumed jobs and retains the latest status across restarts.

Delta-friendly installer packaging, in-place replacement, cached delta bases
and permanent Android signing continue. All publication gates remain required.
See docs/releases/v1.0.8.md for scope and physical-device validation limits.
