Nerqo native 1.0.9 Stable channel

Android user-started foreground transfer service, notification controls and
checkpoints; temporary Disconnect/Reconnect separated from Forget; pinned
identity checks before moving paused jobs to a new private address.

Durable verified-download receipt retries, History file/open/retry/log clearing,
Windows measured per-file send/receive controls and optional no-copy source
sharing, plus Android 16 public 5 GHz hotspot band requests where supported.

Native publication gates, previous-version replacement, delta reconstruction,
permanent Android signing and independent payload checks remain required.
Physical radio/Doze/throughput acceptance and trusted Windows Authenticode
signing are not claimed. See docs/releases/v1.0.9.md.
