Nerqo native 1.0.6 Stable channel

Windows WPF and Android Compose. This update adds portrait QR scanning,
consent-based Wi-Fi QR joining, a Windows hotspot settings shortcut,
Disconnect on both devices and quiet Android background refresh.
Publication requires all build, transport, UI and signing gates to pass.

Windows hotspot creation is controlled by Windows settings. Its Wi-Fi QR
requires a supporting Windows 11 version. Native Wi-Fi Direct/Bluetooth,
in-app frequency selection and separate transfer history remain pending.
Physical-device acceptance and Windows Authenticode signing remain outstanding.
See docs/releases/v1.0.6.md and docs/NATIVE_ROADMAP.md.
